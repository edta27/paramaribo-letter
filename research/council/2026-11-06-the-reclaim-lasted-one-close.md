## Round 2 — risk red team

The confirmation bias is the window. This desk wrote the 48-hour test in advance, and the test failed. That is a score, not a license to skip to $72k or $57k. The next rule was also written in advance: a close under $77k without a 48-hour reclaim. 2 November already showed that the first half can print and the second half can fail the next day. 5 November is another first half. Grading it as finished on the morning of 6 November would be moving the test because the print arrived.

The opposite bias is the one-day reclaim. $80,428 on 13,199 BTC is a real close. Erasing it because 5 November failed is hindsight. Keeping it as a repair is also hindsight in the other direction. Repair still requires the close to stick, and $86k was not touched.

5 November can be promoted into a panic because the VIX did what the definition asks of volatility and the indexes fell together, including equal-weight. The parts that do not fit have to stay in the sentence: January Brent rose, DVOL did not jump with the VIX, OAS was not retrieved, HYG eased 0.3%, and it is one session. A cluster that is declared because it was expected is the error Issue 32 named.

The leverage story from the internal dailies does not survive the OKX buckets. Coin OI was higher at the end of the reclaim bucket, not lower. The account ratio did fall. Without liquidation dollars and without the Binance book, "short squeeze" is a label, not a measurement. The give-back bucket's −4.9% coin OI is a deleveraging of one venue's daily stamp, and the live ratio at 2.88 says the accounts did not stay de-risked.

The missing Farside table will be filled in by whatever story the reader already believes. That is why the letter leaves it blank. The Binance wildcard has the same shape. A stale suspension notice and an unread later post are not symmetrical facts. The letter says unresolved.

The election, the AI headline, and the oil bounce are three stories that can be forced into one paragraph. The tape does not require that. Two cash sessions went opposite ways after the AP call. Oil's same-contract path is down on the week and up on the day Bitcoin and equities fell. Do not merge them into a cause.

Cycle numerology, a slide of the window out to December, and a conditional PENGU target are refused. The October-through-mid-November window is not clean. That grades the speed hypothesis as a failed reclaim of one door, plus one equity-vol day, not as a date.
