## Round 2 — red team

**risk_red_team.** Most damaging error: calling a thin Sunday close a reclaim, or calling a 3.9% six-hour jobs-day the death of H-COMPRESSION. Both are overfit. Second error: treating Friday as “unexpected” in the wild-card sense — it was on the calendar. Third: anniversary gravity at day 364. Fourth: ETF +$190M ≠ +$999M. Fifth: OKX OI is no longer grinding down; “clean leverage” is a fading sentence.

The attack allows a small weight shift toward Model A because $82k held through a real shock and $86k printed once. It forbids electing repair. Keep WAIT. Keep tail 8. Keep the window. If 10 October is quiet, that is not a kill; if mid-November is quiet, that is.

## Synthesis

Question: can a shock travel fast enough, and is there a bid to absorb it?

Answer: Friday traveled in hours (not 10 minutes) and the $82–83k book absorbed it. That is one sample. One Sunday close above $86k is not two. Yields are worse. The experiment continues.
