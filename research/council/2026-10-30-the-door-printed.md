## Round 2 — risk red team

The confirmation bias is real. This desk has been watching $80k for two issues. A close through it will feel like the theory working. The rule that was written in advance has two halves. Only one has printed. Dropping the 48-hour clause now would be moving the test because the first print arrived.

The ETF arithmetic can be overfit in the calm direction. +$440.8M over four days is real, and +$17.2M on the break day is also real. A small positive print is not a bid that held the level. The same error, in the other direction, was available on 22 October's −$635.6M, and Issue 31 already named it.

The Binance number can be overfit into a systemic event. About $6.3B is the company's estimate of assets affected, not a verified liquidation, not a depeg, and not a reserve audit. The opposite error is treating "user funds are safe" as an observed fact. It is a statement. Withdrawals are suspended. That is the observable. Attribution to a state actor is reported and unconfirmed by the company. It does not change the tape by itself.

Credit can be overfit into an all-clear. HY at 3.14% and CCC back at 13.07% are 28 October prints. The break was 29 October. The file does not contain the day that matters. VIX at 17.20 is a completed fact. The live 18.68 is not.

OKX is one venue. The account ratio is not size. The daily bucket may be open. Binance OI was not retrieved, which matters more on a day when Binance is the incident. Missing liquidation dollars stay missing. A round trip from $81,890 to $77,699 is not a forecast of the next round trip.

Cycle numerology, M2 timing, and a slide of the October-through-mid-November window out to December are refused. The October tape is not clean. That grades the speed hypothesis as a one-door break, not as a reason to invent the next date.
