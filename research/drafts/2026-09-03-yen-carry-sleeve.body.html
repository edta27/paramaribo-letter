Central answer

Working answer (desk, Round 2): Bitcoin is not a yen-carry beta sleeve on the average tape right now. Dollar / DXY beta is the clearer daily link. The yen channel is episodic — it can still fire under disorder — but it is not sizeable as a standing sleeve into the BOJ meeting on this packet.

Keep three mechanisms separate:

  1. JPY funding / carry — live under disorder (Aug 2024 n=1; Aug 2026 >2% dip); not the daily driver in return space now.
  2. Dollar / global-risk beta — stronger in the corr panel; fits Sep 3 (softer DXY, BTC up) better than “strong yen → sell BTC.”
  3. JGB level / fiscal shock — the new 2026 spine; can hit risk budgets without a clean one-hop FX→BTC trade.
Do not confuse a BOJ calendar date with a tradable yen-carry beta.
