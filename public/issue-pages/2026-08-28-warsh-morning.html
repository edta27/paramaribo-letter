Warsh morning: Nvidia is done, the box is not

Thursday was an AI session. Friday is a speech. Bitcoin ~$79,800 inside $70k–$82k. Full bench, each lens on its own line, cash until a Daily close.

August 28, 2026 · The Paramaribo Letter

Friday 28 August, about 12:45 a.m. Central — U.S. cash is closed. Thursday was an Nvidia day. Today is a Warsh morning. Bitcoin is still inside the desk’s $70,000–$82,000 box. This letter is a full-bench read on one live packet before the 10:00 a.m. Eastern Jackson Hole keynote. It is not sixteen separate workers in one window. Cash is a valid state.

The tape into the speech

Bitcoin last printed near $79,800 on CoinGecko, up about 1.4 percent over twenty-four hours, with a range of $78,620 to $81,282. Dominance held near 59 percent. Ether against Bitcoin sat around 0.0313. The October 2025 high at $126,080 is still more than thirty-six percent away. Seven-day gain has cooled to about six percent.

Thursday’s equity close: Nasdaq Composite 26,541 (+1.57%), S&P 500 7,731 (+0.72%), Dow 53,569 (+0.2%). Nvidia finished about $227.98 (+8.7%) after the $96.2 billion quarter and $108 billion guide. Coinbase was firm. WhiteFiber stayed soft. The ten-year was near 4.67%. VIX near 14.5. Gold quiet. Dollar around 99.2.

On the meme sleeve, Solana was still hot overnight. Cash Cat, which had ripped on the reclaim, was red about four percent — the first honest cooldown after the fakeout risk this desk already named. Hash ribbons, Energy Value, SOPR, funding, open interest, and Friday’s ETF print were not in the packet.

Warsh is two-sided volume. Nvidia already answered. Do not market-buy $80,000 because Thursday was green.

Bang’s plan (still observation)

His Golden Fib near 77.2k–77.45k was tagged. His long limit was still unfilled when that zone printed. Spot then probed toward $81k. That is price in the observation neighborhood — not confirmation, and not your fill. Desk confirmation remains a Daily close back above $80,000 while $70,000 holds, or a Daily close below $70,000 if the structure fails. Wider observe if Wave C extends: 71.7k–72.4k. Until a close decides, just observe.

How the day and the weekend look

Through the Warsh print and the weekend, council weights stay roughly twenty / fifty / thirty, bull / base / bear. Base case: chop inside the box; speech is theater until the next Daily; no dated crash. Bull: two Daily closes above $82,000 after an easy speech. Bear: Daily loses 77.2k without reclaim, then walks the seventies. September’s larger map — buybacks from 9 September versus FOMC mid-month — is unchanged and still not a prophecy.
