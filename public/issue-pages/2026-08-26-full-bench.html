The bench on Bang, hours before Nvidia

Sixteen lenses, one disagreement: wait for 71.7–72.4k, or let PCE and Warsh decide. Pre-positioning is the error.

August 26, 2026 · The Paramaribo Letter

This is a moderator synthesis, not sixteen separate workers in one window. The question was Bang’s post against a live packet: Bitcoin near $79,000, down about two percent, with dogs already red five to twelve percent, and Nvidia plus PCE a few hours out.

Where the bench agreed: the 78.5k short is closed. Cash and observe are valid. Memes are already flushing harder than Bitcoin. Hash ribbons, funding, and ETF prints for the session were not in the packet.

Where they did not: a completed five-wave is not a law. Macro can gap through 72.4k on a hawkish print, or skip the pullback if guidance and Warsh are easy. Bitwise and the meme lens noted that WIF and PEPE can finish their flush before Bitcoin ever tags value.

The red-team cut

The leading thesis — wait for 71.7–72.4k, then bounce — is correlated with this desk’s older $72,000–$75,500 observe band. The damage is the calendar. PCE, Nvidia, and Warsh sit inside twelve to seventy-two hours. That can make the bounce untradeable, or never print. The attack does not kill observe. It kills getting in early.

Weights through 16 September

Bull 20: never tags 72.4k; Daily holds above $80,000 through Warsh — another missed 62.2k. Base 50: tag 71.7–75.5k this week; $70,000 holds; no dated crash. Bear 30: events slice through 72.4k; Daily loses $70,000.

Levels to keep on one card: 71.7, 72.4, 70, 80. Reassess after Warsh on 28 August.
