September is a box, not a date

Bang tagged 77.2k and left the long unfilled. Nvidia ripped. Warsh is tomorrow. The month ahead is still $70k–$82k two-way.

August 27, 2026 · The Paramaribo Letter

By mid-afternoon on 27 August the question was no longer whether Nvidia had printed. It had. The question was what September looks like from here — with Warsh still ahead, Labor Day a few sessions away, and @BangXBT’s one-hour chart sitting on a Golden Fib that his long limit had not filled.

This is a moderator full-bench synthesis on a live packet, not sixteen separate workers in one window. Spot Bitcoin was near $79,965, up about two percent on the day, with a twenty-four hour range of $78,233 to $80,793. Dominance was still fifty-nine percent. The October 2025 high at $126,080 remained more than thirty-six percent away. The desk box from Issue 01 — $70,000 to $82,000 — still held.

What Bang actually said

His post, about ten hours before the afternoon packet, was process, not a filled trade. The marked observation zone — Golden Fib near 77.2k to 77.45k — had been tagged. Wave A, on his label, was done. He expected Wave B. The long limit had not filled. The chart sketched a bounce, then a lower path into early September toward the low seventies. A quote from 26 August still said the same thing: observe from 77.2k, wait for confirmation, scalp only with signals.

The zone came. The fill did not. Eighty thousand is not the entry.

Elliott on one hour after a squeeze is a label. Kill the certainty with two Daily closes above $82,000. Keep the zone. Drop the calendar prophecy.

Stocks were an Nvidia day

Cash equities at the afternoon tape: Nvidia $227.98, up about 8.7 percent off the beat. Nasdaq led. The S&P was up less than a percent. Broadcom followed. Coinbase and the miner names were mixed to green. WhiteFiber was still red. The ten-year was near 4.67 percent. Gold was firm. The dollar was flat. That is tech risk-on into Warsh, not proof that equities and Bitcoin are the same trade for September.

How September looks from here

Through 1 September the base case is chop: Warsh on the 28th at 10 a.m. Eastern, then a weekend. Weights: twenty / fifty-five / twenty-five, bull / base / bear. Through 30 September the base case is still two-way trade inside $70,000 to $82,000 — buybacks that start on 9 September as duration beta, not QE, against the FOMC on 15–16 September. Weights: twenty-five / forty-five / thirty. Bang’s Wave C toward 71.7k–72.4k fits the base path. It is not a dated crash. A Daily close below $70,000 is the bear line. Two Daily closes above $85,000 break the box the other way.

Cash Cat was up about seventeen percent on the reclaim day. That is late beta, not a September thesis. Hash ribbons, Energy Value, SOPR, funding, and the session’s ETF print were not in the packet. Until those exist, cash remains the overlay, not a blended long.
