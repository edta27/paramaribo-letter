A second probe of eighty

Monday afternoon retested the round number. A wick is not a Daily close. Cash Cat ripped anyway.

August 24, 2026 · The Paramaribo Letter

By Monday afternoon Bitcoin had recovered from the Sunday shake and printed near $79,000, with a twenty-four hour high just under $80,000. The box from the first issue — $70,000 to $82,000 — still held.

That is a second look at the gate. It is not acceptance. The UTC Daily had not closed through $80,000 on the four-hour evidence we had. The observation zone at $72,000–$75,500 still had not been fully tagged; the weekend low only nicked the high seventies.

What moved

Bitcoin was up a couple of percent from the Monday morning packet. Ether and Solana followed. The dogs were mixed to soft. Cash Cat was the exception: up about fifty percent in twenty-four hours while Bitcoin was barely green. That is thin-name catch-up, not a cult, and it is the first name that usually fakeouts into an event week.

Still waiting

Confirmation remains a Daily close above $80,000 with $70,000 intact — or a Daily close below $70,000 for risk-off. Next dated prints: Nvidia and PCE on the 26th, Warsh on the 28th.
