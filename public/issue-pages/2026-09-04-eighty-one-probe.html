Central answer

Working answer (desk): this is a probe, not a confirmed stall break. Two gates, not one. A Daily close above $80k is only bias. Clearing the stall means Daily acceptance through the old failure zone $81–81.5k with $80k still holding on retests. Tonight’s $82.1k is an intraday tag into supply. The same 24h also printed $77.1k. Range expansion is not quiet confirmation.

Weaker dollar can bid this bounce without proving structure. Funding is calm. On-chain is insufficient. Do not upgrade on a wick.
