The failed windows can be overfit. This desk has been watching a ladder of doors for several issues. A window that closes under the line will feel like the theory working. The rule that was written in advance did fail, for $72,000, after $77,000 and $74,000 had already failed. That part is a score. What is not a score is the idea that the 15 November low fails because the written doors failed. The 15 November low was a wick the same day’s close rejected, up at $65,315. Grading 19 November’s $61,913 as a finished breakdown of a shelf this letter did not write down, before 21 November, repeats the error Issue 34 refused to make on the morning of 13 November. The margin is $32.94.

The other error is the wick. $61,161 is a real trade, in a real hour, on a day that had already done more than 4,000 BTC by the daily pull. Calling it nothing, because the candle has not closed, would be a dodge. Calling it acceptance is the opposite dodge.
