Bitcoin Support and Resistance Levels: The Map From $57.7k to $87k

The letter maps Bitcoin as a ladder of levels. Each one is a door: a daily close through it changes the odds, a wick through it does not.

22 letters · updated September 26, 2026

The letter reads Bitcoin price as a confirmation road: a ladder of support and resistance levels, each with a rule for what confirms it and what invalidates it. The core discipline is that a wick is not a daily close. A level only counts as broken when price closes through it and fails to reclaim it.

As of Issue 27 (26 September 2026), the ladder runs from the $87.4k sweep that was rejected, through $85k to $86k (lost as a holding zone), $82k to $83k (tested and held on a daily close), $80k, $77k, the $74k to $76k process band, $71k to $72k near the 50-day average, and the 2026 low near $57,700. Below that, $38k is kept only as a severe-tail reference. Weekly structure matters too: May's $82,792 weekly high was a key test through early September.

The rule for reading the ladder is sequence. Relief rallies and lower highs can appear anywhere, but a bearish path has to fail each door in turn with rising intensity, and a bullish path has to hold daily closes while leverage resets and spot demand returns. Skipping straight from $77k to $28k in the headlines is exactly what the letter tries not to do.

The issues below show the map as it was drawn at each point in time, so you can check what was written down in advance against what happened.
