From data to decision

Four gates before a call is published

Every stage can stop with “insufficient evidence.” Cash and patience remain valid outcomes.

  1. 01

    Observe

    Assemble price, flows, positioning, on-chain, macro and cross-market evidence at one timestamp.

  2. 02

    Interpret alone

    Each relevant lens writes independently before seeing the others, limiting narrative herding.

  3. 03

    Challenge

    The red team attacks the leading explanation and names the print that would invalidate it.

  4. 04

    Publish

    The editor separates facts, interpretation and speculation into scenarios with decision rules.

Required second round

The red team tries to break the conclusion.

It does not produce another market prediction. It looks for omitted evidence, circular reasoning, stale data and a story that cannot be falsified.

“What observable print would prove this thesis wrong?”
