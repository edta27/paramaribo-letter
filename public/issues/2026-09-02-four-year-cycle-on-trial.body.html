Central question — rhyme, law, or new regime?

Working answer tonight (desk): history is only rhyming. The four-year playbook is a weak prior from a tiny sample — useful as a prompt, not as a law. Spot ETFs and larger institutional plumbing are real structural changes; they can shallow or amplify drawdowns depending on flows we have not pulled. The low-$60ks → ~$80k rebound is insufficient evidence to call either “new bull impulse” or “classic bear-market relief” with high confidence. Best near-term model remains the published stall / process map, not a dated Q4 prophecy.

Cycle falsification (before the weights)

Best analogue (weakly): a post-peak digestion year with a deep drawdown already printed (low-$60ks) and a rebound into resistance — closer in shape to parts of prior post-ATH years than to a clean mid-bull pause. That is rhyme, not identification.

Worst analogue: treating 2018 as a calendar template for “must dump in Q4 from here.” 2018 had different liquidity, no spot ETF complex of today’s size, and a different rate regime. Forcing 2026 candles into 2018’s story is narrative fit.

Sample size: a handful of prior “four-year” observations is not a density. Apparent periodicity can partly be chance, halving folklore, and shared macro shocks. Killa’s first duty stands: the cycle forecast is statistically fragile.

What distinguishes “$60ks were the bottom” from “$60→$80 was a bear rally” tonight? On this packet: almost nothing decisive. Bulls have the +24%-ish thirty-day bounce and the unreclaimed-but-still-above-$70k structure. Bears have the failed $81–81.5k, the live $77k test, and soft alts. On-chain / ETF / vol that would adjudicate are insufficient.

ETFs: can weaken cycle magnitude (deeper structural bid) or amplify a decline if creations reverse into sustained redemptions. Both mechanisms are live in theory; neither is measured in tonight’s pull. Do not pick the flattering one.

September → Q4: seasonality from a tiny sample is calendar bias until it is a structure break. Crowded “correction soon” can reduce surprise or worsen a gap if magnitude is underestimated — again, OI/funding missing.

Macro stack: elevated equities, yields, debt, geopolitics, and leverage are often correlated versions of duration / risk appetite. Double-counting them as independent crash engines is Issue 16’s red-team kill. Vulnerability ≠ probability. No financial-system stress series is in hand — only narratives.
