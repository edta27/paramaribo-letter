The See’s test, applied without hindsight

What was knowable in 1972: a regional boxed-chocolate business earning a high return on a small tangible base, sold for cash, with a manager in the building. The mistake Buffett later confessed was a framework that treated ~3× tangible assets as a hard stop. The sellers caved. That luck is not a 2026 trading signal.

What does not transfer: “therefore always pay up,” or “therefore $79k is cheap because Bitcoin is See’s.” See’s incremental capital was tiny. Bitcoin’s path to $46k or $100k is a liquidity / positioning / rates path. The transferable habit is: do not grade tonight’s wait with a print that does not exist yet — and do not treat a deep crash node as the only honest buy.

Two different waits are on the table. They must not be mashed.

  1. Process wait: cash / observe until a Daily close decides the $77–80k stall (Issues 13–14). Compatible with Bang’s close-first rule.
  2. Target wait: refuse to participate until ~$46k. That is a crash-path bet. Its expectancy depends on nodes that have not printed.
