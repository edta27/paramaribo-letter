Observed: an identifiable macro shock traveled through Bitcoin in hours, not in the ten-minute hole Issue 28 reconstructed for 10 October 2025. Peak to trough on 2 October was about $3,399 / 3.9% in 375 minutes on the 5-minute Coinbase tape. The $82,000 door was not tagged. The afternoon low was $83,850. The daily close was $84,505. Four sessions later, 4 October produced the first completed close above $86k since 22 September — on Sunday volume of 2,623 BTC.

Calculated: from the 28 September low of $82,510 to the 2 October high of $87,249 is about +5.7% in four days. Using completed closes through 4 October, SMA20 / SMA50 / SMA100 / SMA200 were approximately $82,856 / $79,026 / $71,267 / $71,502. RSI14 (Wilder) was 68.5. Inferred: speed and absorption are both in the tape, and they are not the same fact. The event was fast enough to matter. The book was deep enough, in this instance, to hold the named near-term door. That does not prove the book will hold a larger shock, a thinner session, or a second close above $86k. The desk decision remains WAIT.

5. Was there enough liquidity to absorb it?

In this instance, at the $82–83k door: yes. The flush stopped $1,850 above $82,000. Spot volume on the rejection day was real (8,762 BTC). Weekend follow-through was not a cascade. By 4 October the market was printing above $86k again.

That is not the same as proving depth at $80k, $77k, or $60k. Those books were not tested. Order-book depth, CME basis, and a primary liquidation heatmap remain MISSING. Venue bid/ask at this freeze is a one-cent Coinbase spread; that is a snapshot, not a depth series.

What can be said without the missing plumbing: the near-term bid that Issue 26 named in advance has now absorbed three probes (24 Sep $82,709; 28 Sep $82,510; 2 Oct $83,850). Absorption at one gate is evidence about that gate. It is not a general proof that “there is enough liquidity.”

11. Why this reading could be wrong

The Sunday $86,507 close can be overfit into “reclaim.” Volume argues otherwise. A second close above $86k today or tomorrow would make this issue look late, not clever. ETF creations of +$190M are not the +$999M impulse of 21 September; treating them as equivalent would repeat Issue 27’s slope mistake in reverse. Yields are higher than at the last freeze — the jobs print did not clear the hurdle rate. OKX OI has started to rebuild; “leverage is clean” is less true than it was on 29 September, even if it is still not October 2025. Dominance at 59.2% can be Bitcoin bid or altcoin stress; this packet cannot separate them. Cycle day 364 is how anniversary thinking sneaks back in. Refuse it.

The other error is the bear one: deciding that because Friday was only 3.9% and six hours, H-COMPRESSION is dead. One absorbed shock does not prove the next book is thick. October 2025 was a different machine with more fuel. This week showed the speed half of the question can print without the cascade half.
