9. Why this reading could be wrong

The outflow can be overfit. Since Issue 30 the completed ETF window still nets about +$357 million. A creation day once today’s Farside row is real would make 22 October look like the return of a bid that blinked, which is a sentence this desk has already used. Treating −$635.6 million as the start of a structural break ignores that arithmetic.

The other error is the calm one. Four closes under $82k, a lower high at $84,856, open interest up on the down day, and accounts at 2.21 are a worse book than Issue 30 described, even with $80k intact. VIX at 19 is not the 16.04 this letter’s predecessor saw at 9:40 a.m. Calling the bond rally an all-clear ignores oil, the Nasdaq, and a volatility channel that has been open since the 16 October close.

The account ratio is not positioning. Binance was not retrieved. Liquidation dollars were not retrieved. A round trip from $84,856 to $80,790 is not a measured cascade, and it is not a forecast of one. Dominance at 59.4% can be Bitcoin bid or alt stress.
