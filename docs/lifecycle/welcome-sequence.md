Thanks for subscribing to The Paramaribo Letter.

Educational scenario research only. This is not personalized financial advice. No execution instructions, leverage recommendations, or position sizing.

## Message 2 — after 3 days

**Subject:** How to read a Paramaribo Letter

The short version: start with the evidence packet, then read the disagreement.

Named traders and firms are analytical lenses, not authors of this publication. Scenarios are conditional, levels are observations rather than promises, and a forecast is only useful if its invalidation is clear.

Browse the archive: https://www.paramariboletter.com/index.html#archive-wrap

**Subject:** Keep the archive, question the certainty

Every issue stays available so readers can compare what was said with what happened next. The goal is a useful record of reasoning, not a stream of guaranteed calls.
